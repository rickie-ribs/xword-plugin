# Clue-type benchmarks

Share of clues by type in published NYT puzzles: 91,290 clues from 1,097 puzzles, Nov 2023 to Nov 2026. A rule-based classifier (punctuation and keywords, not meaning) sorted them, so treat each share as roughly ±2 points. "Proper noun" covers names, places, brands and titles; show titles in quotes land under quote/exclamation.

| Type | All | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain definition | 63% | 66% | 64% | 63% | 63% | 63% | 62% |
| Proper noun | 15.5% | 15% | 15% | 16% | 16% | 14% | 15% |
| Fill-in-the-blank | 6.2% | 7.6% | 6.9% | 6.3% | 5.5% | 5.5% | 5.7% |
| Quote or exclamation | 5.6% | 5% | 6% | 6% | 5% | 6% | 6% |
| Wordplay (ends in "?") | 4.5% | 1.1% | 2.8% | 4.1% | 5.0% | 6.6% | 6.4% |
| Abbreviation signal | 3.2% | 3% | 3% | 3.5% | 3.4% | 2.7% | 3.2% |
| Cross-reference | 1.1% | 1.3% | 1.3% | 1.6% | 1.1% | 0.5% | 0.7% |
| Foreign-language hint | 0.6% | 0.9% | 0.6% | 0.5% | 0.5% | 0.7% | 0.7% |

Sunday looks like the all-days column (wordplay 5.2%, fill-in 5.9%).

## Other findings

- Clues average 4.0 words; 6% run past 8 words (Saturday: 7.5%).
- Wordplay is the only type that moves with the day: about 1% Monday, about 6.5% Friday and Saturday.
- Cross-references are mostly theme or revealer clues ("hint to 17-, 26-, 49-Across"). Non-theme "See N-Across" clues are rare.
- Fill-ins are Monday's second most common type after definitions.
- Names and fill-ins often combine ("Dien Bien ___, 1954 battle site in Vietnam").

## How to use

Compare the puzzle's non-theme clues to the column for its intended day. Report a type only when it is several points off, and say the counts are rough. Do not call a puzzle thin on wordplay if it matches the Monday or Tuesday figure. In a 72-clue puzzle a 1-point gap is under one clue.

The percentages describe published puzzles, not a target: a type that matches the norm needs no fix.
